## Required governance artifacts

- AI/system inventory with purpose, owner, users, affected parties, data, model, version, dependencies, deployment and status.
- RACI and separation-of-duties matrix.
- Intended-use, prohibited-use, human-oversight, override, contest and redress policy.
- Model acceptance/change/retraining/rollback/retirement criteria.
- Supplier/component due diligence, provenance, vulnerability and license process.
- Risk acceptance template with authority, evidence, expiry and monitoring condition.
- Incident classification and notification matrix covering security, privacy, data/model integrity, bias and harmful outcomes.

## Decision gates

1. **Concept:** purpose, affected parties, alternatives, initial risks and accountable owner approved.
2. **Data:** authority, minimization, lineage, quality and use restrictions approved.
3. **Model:** independent validation and threshold exceptions approved.
4. **Deployment:** security/privacy controls, monitoring, rollback and user experience assessed.
5. **Operation:** periodic review and material-change triggers active.
6. **Retirement:** access removed, artifacts/data retained or disposed according to policy, downstream use addressed.
