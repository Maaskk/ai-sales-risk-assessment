# EBIOS Risk Manager Method Notes

EBIOS RM combines a security baseline with scenario analysis and proceeds iteratively from business missions to operational attack paths and treatment.

## Workshop 1 — Scope and security baseline

- **Objective:** define the studied object, participants, timeframe, business values, supporting assets, feared events and severity; evaluate the applicable security baseline.
- **Inputs:** business mission, stakeholder context, architecture/data knowledge, requirements and policies.
- **Questions:** what matters, what supports it, what unacceptable event could happen, how serious would it be, and which baseline deviations need attention?
- **Outputs:** scope, values/assets, feared events, severity, baseline gaps.
- **Feeds:** Workshop 2 objectives and Workshop 3 target/impact paths.

## Workshop 2 — Risk sources

- **Objective:** pair relevant risk sources with objectives and assess relevance.
- **Inputs:** Workshop 1 business values/feared events plus threat and ecosystem context.
- **Questions:** who could act, what would they seek, what motivation/resources/access do they have, and why is the pair relevant?
- **Outputs:** prioritized risk-source/objective pairs.
- **Feeds:** Workshop 3 strategic scenarios.

## Workshop 3 — Strategic scenarios

- **Objective:** show how a risk source could use ecosystem paths to reach the studied object and cause a feared event.
- **Inputs:** prioritized source/objective pairs, ecosystem dependencies, business values.
- **Questions:** which stakeholder/dependency is exposed, how dependable is it, and which path reaches the target?
- **Outputs:** ecosystem map, strategic scenarios, security measures for the ecosystem.
- **Feeds:** Workshop 4 technical operational paths.

## Workshop 4 — Operational scenarios

- **Objective:** construct technical attack paths, evaluate plausibility, and identify detection/prevention evidence.
- **Inputs:** strategic scenarios, architecture/DFD, threat intelligence, existing controls.
- **Questions:** what sequence of actions and assets is needed, what capability/access is required, how plausible is it, and what evidence supports control claims?
- **Outputs:** operational scenarios, plausibility, attack-path diagrams, control gaps.
- **Feeds:** Workshop 5 treatment prioritization.

**My own explanation:** _Write this in your own words before presenting._

## Workshop 5 — Risk treatment

- **Objective:** choose avoid/reduce/share/accept responses, plan measures, determine residual risk, and establish continuous improvement.
- **Inputs:** feared-event severity, operational plausibility, existing/planned measures, risk appetite.
- **Questions:** what response is justified, who owns it, what evidence proves completion, what residual risk remains, and who may accept it?
- **Outputs:** treatment plan, residual-risk register, roadmap, monitoring and acceptance decisions.
